# Project Memory

## Core
Brand: JKTechSolutions (Perth, WA). IT support for SMEs (5-50 staff) & home.
Style: Navy Trust palette (#0f1b3d deep navy, #3b6fa0 blue accent, #e8edf3 off-white). Libre Baskerville serif headings + IBM Plex Sans body. Light base with dark navy Services/Contact sections. Sharp corners, subtle shadows. No cyan, no neon.
Contact Rule: NEVER show phone number or business hours. Email (.com.au) only.
CTA Rule: Primary CTA is 'Free IT Health Check' or '15-min call'. NEVER generic 'Get Started'.
Trust Signals: 'Degree Qualified', sub-60 min response, no lock-in contracts.
