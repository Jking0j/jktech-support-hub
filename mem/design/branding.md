# JKTechSolutions — Navy Trust design system

Palette (locked):
- Deep navy #0f1b3d — primary brand, dark section backgrounds
- Mid navy #1e3a5f — body copy on light bg, subtle surfaces on dark
- Blue accent #3b6fa0 — hyperlinks, eyebrow labels, small accents
- Off-white #e8edf3 — main light background & text on dark

Typography:
- Headings: Libre Baskerville (serif, italic used for emphasis)
- Body: IBM Plex Sans

Style rules:
- Light off-white base with deep navy ink. Dark navy for Services + Contact sections.
- Sharp corners (radius 0.25rem), thin borders, quiet shadows.
- Subtle fade/slide reveals only. No neon glows, no cyan, no gradients-as-focus.
- Uppercase tracked-widest eyebrows for section labels.
- Logo mark: navy square with "JK" in serif.
